Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Bluebank is a free, non-commercial practice tool for the official College Board question bank. You do not need an account to use it.

If you do not sign in

Bluebank collects nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking and no advertising anywhere on the site. Your practice history is stored only in your own browser. Google's sign-in code is not even downloaded unless you open the sync panel and click sign in.

The one request your browser makes

The questions themselves come from College Board. Your browser asks them directly, so College Board sees your IP address the way any site you visit does. Bluebank has no server in the middle of that and stores no question data.

Opening the on-screen calculator loads it from Desmos, and only then. If you never open it, your browser never contacts Desmos.

If you do sign in

Signing in with Google syncs your practice across your devices. What gets stored on the server is:

  • which questions you answered, what you answered, and how long you took
  • your highlights and notes
  • which questions you flagged
  • your mistake log entries
  • your practice sets

We do not store your email address, your name or your profile picture. Google gives us an anonymous account identifier, and that identifier is the only thing your practice history is attached to. Nothing is ever sold, shared or passed to a third party.

Deleting your data

The sync panel on the Stats page has a delete button that erases everything held on the server for your account, permanently and immediately. It does not touch the copy in your own browser, so deleting your server data never costs you your practice history.

To clear the copy in your browser, clear this site's data the same way you clear cookies.

Children

Bluebank is intended for students preparing for the SAT. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
